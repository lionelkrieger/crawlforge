## Rationale

D-010 makes CrawlForge open source; D-011 selects AGPL-3.0 with a CLA. Excluded subsystem §3 row 2 (no licence, subscription, trial or seat concepts) remains correct: it prohibits licensing *clients and entitlement state*, which open source does not need. §8.9 requires "permissive open-source license compliance" for bundled dependencies; that must be reconciled with the product's own copyleft licence.

## Proposed text

**New subsection `1.4 Product licence` (FS-CRAWL-001 numbering continues in this file):**

> - CrawlForge is distributed as open source under the **GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0)**. The repository carries a `LICENSE` file with the full licence text and a licence notice in its README.
> - The licence is a property of the distributed source and binaries. It introduces **no** licence keys, activation, entitlement state, account or licence check of any kind; §3 rows 2, 3 and 8 remain in force.
> - Outside contributions are accepted only under a **contributor licence agreement (CLA)** that grants the project owner the right to relicense the contribution. A CLA check runs on the repository before any contribution is merged.
> - Every bundled dependency and data asset must have a licence compatible with distribution under AGPL-3.0. Compatibility is verified by the licence audit in section 09-distribution before each public release.

**§8.9, current bullet:**

> - Permissive open-source license compliance; a local attribution file ships with the bundle.

**Replacement:**

> - Bundled third-party libraries and assets must carry licences compatible with distribution under AGPL-3.0 (permissive licences such as MIT and Apache-2.0 qualify); a local attribution file listing every bundled component and its licence ships with the bundle.
